# Welcome

This post is a welcome to my blog, where I'll work on and write about my own things from now on. I'll also give a short introduction to who I am.  
I'm Romer, a full-stack developer with a degree in Systems Administration from [IES Puerto de la Cruz](https://iespuertodelacruz.es/), in the Canary Islands (Tenerife, Spain). I love working on the premise of writing sustainable code and following the DevOps philosophy.

## How this blog started

I've always believed that one of the deepest ways to learn is to write about what you do and what drives you. Following the advice of [@CarlosBle](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlosble/), I decided to start my own blog, where I'll simply write for myself about what interests me, above all sustainable code and software architecture.
